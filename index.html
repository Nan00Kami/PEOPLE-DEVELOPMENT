Human-Scale Development for a Changing World.

Grounded Realities. Open Data. Democratic Futures.

People & Development is an open digital commons, an active publisher, and a living public space. We bridge grassroots field realities across Kerala and India with the wider global development discourse—breaking down academic gatekeeping through peer-reviewed research, Panchayat-level data sandboxes, audio dispatches, and public roundtables.
