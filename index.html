Human-Scale Development for a Changing World.

Grounded Realities. Open Data. Democratic Futures.

Decades ago, pioneers showed that true progress is measured not by towering structures or abstract economic aggregates, but by human dignity, equitable access, and ecological harmony. Today, People & Development returns as a living, open-access digital commons—connecting grassroots action across Kerala and India with global policy networks.

A Tradition of Questioning, A Future of Building

Carrying the Flame Forward

When four visionaries—C. Achutha Menon, Dr. K.N. Raj, Laurie Baker, and T.R. Chandradutt ("Dutt Mash")—came together to shape the discourse around decentralized planning, cost-effective habitat, and human development, they challenged orthodoxies.

“True development has never been about grand monuments or glossy economic statistics. Solutions must be human-scale, local, and democratically owned.”

We do not look back out of nostalgia; we look back for orientation. The realities confronting us in the present century—erratic climate vulnerability, shifting labour dynamics, aging care economies, and unbridled urbanization—demand the same plain-spoken courage, equipped with contemporary tools.
