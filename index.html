Human-Scale Development for a Changing World.

Grounded Realities. Open Data. Democratic Futures.

People & Development is an open-access public interest journalism portal and living knowledge commons. We bridge grassroots field realities across Kerala and India with wider global development debates—investigating climate vulnerability, decentralized governance, primary care, and labor.

Rather than confining critical research behind expensive academic paywalls and dense jargon, we produce continuous field reporting, open Panchayat-level data sandboxes, audio dispatches, and public roundtables.
