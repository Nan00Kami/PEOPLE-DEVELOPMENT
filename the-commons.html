Democratizing Knowledge & Action

True development has never been about grand monuments or glossy economic statistics[cite: 1]. Decades ago, four visionaries—C. Achutha Menon, Dr. K.N. Raj, Laurie Baker, and T.R. Chandradutt ("Dutt Mash")—came together with a shared conviction: that planning, habitat, and science belong in the hands of ordinary people[cite: 1].

Through pioneering efforts that laid the ground for COSTFORD and, later, the Laurie Baker Centre for Habitat Studies, they demystified architecture and economic policy[cite: 1]. They proved that human dignity, ecological sense, and decentralized democracy build far more resilient communities than top-down mandates[cite: 1].

Today, People & Development steps forward as an open digital commons, an active publisher, and a living public space[cite: 1]. You will not find exorbitant academic paywalls or impenetrable jargon here[cite: 1]. Instead, we provide continuous peer-reviewed research, interactive Panchayat-level data sandboxes, audio field dispatches, and open town-hall discussions[cite: 1].
