Remembering the four path-finders whose shared conviction proved that planning, habitat, and science belong in the hands of ordinary people.

The Four Visionaries

Foundations of Decentralized Praxis

C. Achutha Menon

Decentralized Governance

Visionary Chief Minister of Kerala who championed land reforms, decentralized planning, and established premier scientific and research institutions across the state.

Dr. K.N. Raj

Human Development

Economist and founder of the Centre for Development Studies (CDS). Reoriented economic planning toward literacy, healthcare, and human dignity over mere growth aggregates[cite: 1, 2].

Laurie Baker

Ecological Habitat

Demystified architecture by proving that sustainable, low-cost buildings can be made with local materials, passive ventilation, and unplastered brickwork[cite: 1, 2].

T.R. Chandradutt

“Dutt Mash” / COSTFORD

Pioneered civic mobilization and mass science awareness through COSTFORD, turning ecological building into an accessible community movement.

Living Archive Spotlight
“You ask the question of each item however small: is it necessary? If it’s not necessary, don’t do it.”

Excerpt from Vineet Radhakrishnan's documentary featurette Uncommon Sense: The Life & Architecture of Laurie Baker. Documenting Baker’s radical deviation from standard architectural extravagance in favor of small, durable homes built for 250 sq ft and accessible budgets.
