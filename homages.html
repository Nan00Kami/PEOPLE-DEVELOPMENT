Remembering the four path-finders whose shared conviction proved that planning, habitat, and science belong in the hands of ordinary people.

The Four Visionaries

Foundations of Decentralized Praxis

C. Achutha Menon

Decentralized Governance

Visionary Chief Minister of Kerala who championed land reforms, institutional building, and democratic decentralization.

Dr. K.N. Raj

Human Development

Founder of the Centre for Development Studies (CDS); reoriented planning toward health and education over mere growth aggregates.

Laurie Baker

Ecological Habitat

Demystified architecture by demonstrating that resilient, cost-effective buildings rely on local materials and passive cooling.

T.R. Chandradutt

“Dutt Mash” / COSTFORD

Pioneered mass civic mobilization through COSTFORD, turning ecological building into an accessible community movement.
