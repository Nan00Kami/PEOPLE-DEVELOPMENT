Remembering the four visionaries who proved that human dignity, material frugality, and decentralized planning belong in the hands of ordinary people[cite: 1].

The Four Path-Finders

C. Achutha Menon

Decentralized Governance

Champion of land reforms, institutional building, and democratic decentralization across Kerala.

Dr. K.N. Raj

Human Development Economics

Founding economist who rooted development planning in grassroots well-being and education.

Laurie Baker

Ecological Habitat

Pioneered low-cost brickwork, energy minimalism, and housing accessible to ordinary working citizens.

T.R. Chandradutt

“Dutt Mash” / COSTFORD

Architect of civic mobilization, translating sustainable construction into practical mass reality.

Documentary Featurette
“We should put all our efforts into a lot of small things rather than in all of us wanting to build one or two big masterpieces... small is beautiful.”

From Vineet Radhakrishnan's film featurette Uncommon Sense: The Life & Architecture of Laurie Baker. Explore Baker’s radical cost reduction, passive jali cooling, waste-material reuse, and his lifelong commitment to housing ordinary people.
