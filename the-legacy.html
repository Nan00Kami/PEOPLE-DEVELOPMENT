A Tradition of Questioning, A Future of Building

Carrying the Flame Forward

Genuine progress is never handed down from above; it is built together, step by step, at a human scale.

Our Core Manifesto

True development has never been about grand monuments or glossy economic statistics. Decades ago, four visionaries—C. Achutha Menon, Dr. K.N. Raj, Laurie Baker, and T.R. Chandradutt ("Dutt Mash")—came together with a shared conviction: that planning, habitat, and science belong in the hands of ordinary people.

Through pioneering efforts that laid the ground for COSTFORD and, later, the Laurie Baker Centre for Habitat Studies, they demystified architecture and economic policy. They proved that human dignity, ecological sense, and decentralized democracy build far more resilient communities than top-down mandates.

People & Development has inherited that spirit of fearless questioning. Today that spirit is not an excursion into nostalgia for us, but our very orientation. The realities confronting us in the present century—erratic climate patterns, precarious labour, an aging society, and unbridled urbanization—demand the same plain-spoken courage.
