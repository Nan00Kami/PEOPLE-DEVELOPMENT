Open Data & Audit SOP

Every dataset hosted in our sandboxes is published under the Creative Commons CC BY-SA 4.0 license. We do not obscure raw sources: every metric links directly to Grama Sabha audits, local body publications, and verifiable field surveys[cite: 1, 2].
